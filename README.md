# AgroNepal – Smart Farmer Platform

AgroNepal is a digital platform designed to modernize the agricultural sector of Nepal by connecting farmers, buyers, and agricultural services in one place. It helps farmers manage their activities, access resources, and stay updated with agricultural trends and events.

---

## Project Overview

AgroNepal aims to bridge the gap between traditional farming practices and modern digital technology. The platform provides tools for farmers to improve productivity, maintain records, and access important agricultural services easily.

---

##  Features

- Farmer Registration & Login
- Farm Record Management
- Expense and sales record.
- Agriculture Event & Ticket Booking
- Community Interaction & Discussion
- Contact & Feedback System
